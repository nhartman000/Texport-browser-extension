# TExporT Markers — Privacy Policy

*Effective 2026-10-07 · American Milestone Inc*

TExporT Markers does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **What it reads:** the text of the conversation page you are on (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, gemini.google.com), and only when you mark a passage or add a conversation to a collection.
- **Where it goes:** into your browser's local extension storage (`storage.local`) on your own device. Nothing is sent to American Milestone Inc or any third party. The extension makes no network requests.
- **Exports:** PDF and Markdown files are created by your browser and saved where you choose.
- **Deleting your data:** remove collections in the extension popup, or uninstall the extension, which deletes its storage.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no remote code.**

Contact: American Milestone Inc, via the support link on the store listing.
