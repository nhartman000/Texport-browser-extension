TExporT Markers — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-20 · American Milestone Inc

TExporT Markers does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

  • What it reads:
    • the text of the AI conversation you're on (claude.ai, chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, gemini.google.com);
    • any other page only when you choose Mark this page for that tab.
    It reads text only when you mark a passage, open the marker window, or add a conversation to a collection.
  • Where it goes: into your browser's local extension storage (storage.local) on your own device. Nothing is sent to American Milestone Inc or any third party. The extension makes no network requests.
  • Smart Marker: TExporT puts a request on your clipboard. Only if you paste it into your chat yourself does the request go to that AI service, the same as anything else you type there. TExporT then reads the answer from the page.
  • Clipboard: TExporT writes to your clipboard only when you use Smart Marker or a Copy button. It never reads your clipboard.
  • Exports: PDF, Markdown and .texport.json files are created by your browser and saved where you choose.
  • Deleting your data: remove markers in the panel and collections in the extension popup, or uninstall the extension, which deletes its storage.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no remote code.

Contact: American Milestone Inc, via https://github.com/nhartman000/Texport-browser-extension/issues
